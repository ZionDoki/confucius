# Appearance settings

English · [简体中文](appearance.zh-CN.md)

[User guide](README.md) · [CLI connections](runtime-discovery.md)

Open **Confucius Settings → Appearance**, or **Zotero Settings → Confucius →
Appearance**. Settings use top tabs. Edit the values, then click **Save** to apply
them to open Confucius workspaces, reading windows and selection-question popups.
Closing or cancelling discards unsaved appearance changes.
While Save is committing, editing and cancellation are temporarily unavailable;
they become available again after success or an error.

- **Font size:** 12–28 px in 0.5 px steps, using a slider or number input.
- **Line height:** 1.2–2.4 in 0.05 steps. This adjusts reading text.
- **Font:** eight offline presets, or search and select an installed font under
  **Installed fonts**. Presets use available local fonts with system fallbacks;
  they do not download fonts. A saved font missing on another computer falls
  back to a system font until you choose another.
- **Theme:** follow Zotero, light, or dark. The light/dark color-editing tabs
  select which palette you edit; they do not switch the active theme.
- **Colors:** choose the warm default or neutral preset, or customize page and
  workspace backgrounds, controls, body text, secondary text and accent. Enter
  `#RRGGBB` or use the color picker. Light and dark colors save independently.

There is no appearance preview. **Restore appearance defaults** changes the draft;
click **Save** to apply it. Defaults remain 13 px, 1.6 line height, system sans
font and the warm palette. Previously saved fonts and line-height presets are
retained when upgrading.

System high contrast takes precedence over custom colors. Changes are scoped to
Confucius surfaces; Zotero's library, PDF pages and other preference panes retain
their own appearance.
